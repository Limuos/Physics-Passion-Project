Brainstorming

Physics Passion Project

A quick overview of our passions and why we're passionate about them!

Soumil's Passions

01

Robotics

I love the concept of engineering and building things for the sake of humanity. It allows me to challenge myself working with different materials, concepts and principles all while developing my technical skills as well. I think my passion for robotics has stemmed from my childhood where I was fascinated by RC cars, gadgets and other games that would allow me to create.

02

Volleyball

I have loved playing volleyball ever since I was a kid. I loved the sport in itself but also a lot of other things about it. I loved the fact that it was a distraction away from everything else going on around me, somewhere where I could just focus on the game. It also taught me discipline and hard work which I apply to my everyday life.

03

Playing Piano

Playing piano has been one of my more recent hobbies that I have developed but something I wish that I had started sooner. It's taught me a lot about creativity as well as concentration when playing different scales, pieces or even songs. It allowed me to see what I can do when I put my mind to something and it's a hobby that I'll always practice.

04

Photography

Photography is one of my newer hobbies but has seemed to be one of my favourites. It's always interesting to see ordinary objects, people and items in a different perspective and that's what I love about photography. It lets me experiment with different lenses and allows me to view things differently which is important throughout life.

05

Rock Climbing

Rock climbing is one of my hobbies that helps me stay physically strong. It allows me to both practice strength training but also visualize things in multiple views. A major part of climbing is trial and error as many times, climbing doesn't go your way and you fall, but it's important that you get back up and try again which applies to way more than just rock climbing.

06

Space

Space has always been interesting to me, the vast cosmos and the everlasting possibility of anything being out there. From the early discoveries of planets to new breakthroughs in lifeforms being found, space travel, exploration and development have always seemed like the future of the human race and its continuance.

Eshaan's Passions

01

Robotics

I've ended up loving robotics because I love the learning that I have done through it. The design principles, the changes to my thinking process because I have learned how to work with people towards a common goal. I think that my love for robotics has become broad and honestly a bit disconnected from the real product. Learning how to CAD has become the single best thing I've learned from robotics, and something I want to integrate into my project.

02

Planes

I play a lot of plane games, and I have been interested in all sorts of machines that use the air to move around, from airlines to the bi-planes of WW1. The biggest part that interests me is the way that different frames manipulate air to have such different characteristics.

03

Cars and ATVs

Since I started playing video games, I've been interested in how humanity has built modes of transportation, and how different needs result in surprisingly different innovations for even the same technology. This has brought me towards cars and the different types of motor vehicles.

04

Karate

I've been practicing karate since I was 5, and I think that it's become more a part of my life than necessarily a passion. When I was 10-14 I really disliked it because it was something hard. I think that now I find keeping up with that difficulty as something that I need to do to keep balance in the rest of my life, so I continue. I've done some tournaments as well.

05

Saxophone

I started playing the saxophone, and haven't considered stopping since. Through the Woodlands Music Program I have met amazing friends, gone to both Chicago and Whistler, BC, and gotten a lot out of learning. Additionally by myself, I've played at retirement homes, and for people's birthdays, so it really has become a rewarding hobby as well.

06

Photography

I started taking photos for my older cousin when I was in 6th grade, and since then I've become obsessed with it myself. Recently I got my mom's iPhone 14 Pro as an upgrade, and since then, taking photos has become something I more often catch myself doing. I don't really know enough about it to call it a hobby, but I've been interested in it recently.
